# Twitter/X and social-media sentiment for short-horizon crypto trading (as of 2026-10-22)

## Q1. Does social sentiment predict crypto returns, volume or volatility at 1h or shorter, after costs?

### Takeaway
Intraday links exist but are small. The strongest evidence shows StockTwits sentiment predicting BTC returns only up to about 15 minutes, with effects too small to trade profitably. Most "predictive" results are at 1 to 4 days, are two-way (price drives sentiment), or come from single-influencer events (Musk on DOGE). Tweet volume and attention predict volatility better than tone predicts direction. For a spot bot with 0.10 to 0.13% fees per side and 1 day left, this is not a reliable source of profit.

### Cited Findings
- StockTwits, about 1M messages, 2017-2019: sentiment is significantly related to Bitcoin returns at frequencies up to 15 minutes. At lower frequencies the relation disappears, and the authors say the effect is too small for a trader to profit from (Kürzinger dissertation, Univ. Düsseldorf) — [Dissertation PDF](https://docserv.uni-duesseldorf.de/servlets/DerivateServlet/Derivate-72975/Dissertation_Kuerzinger.pdf) (figures from the search-result abstract; full text not read)
- Hourly Twitter mood (Loughran-McDonald dictionary) and BTC, Gao/Huang/Wang 2021: bullish sentiment predicts higher returns and volatility over a 24h horizon, controlling for lagged returns and volatility. Bearish and neutral sentiment were not consistently predictive. No cost-adjusted trading result was found — [Virtual Economics / DOAJ](https://doaj.org/article/8103c122807d43f5974f43d4be6a453d)
- Hourly BTC tweets, Sep 2020 to Feb 2021, VAR-BEKK-GARCH: causality runs both ways (price affects sentiment and sentiment affects price). Emotion-volatility spillovers include a one-period lag for fear and anger. This points to "already priced in" and reverse causality — [Yelkenci et al., Studies in Economics and Finance](https://ideas.repec.org/a/eme/sefpps/sef-12-2022-0572.html)
- Explicit buy/sell calls on X, Reddit, StockTwits and Telegram (2025, Electronic Markets) significantly predict short-term crypto moves, mostly for low-cap coins and after recent negative returns. The out-of-sample strategy beat CCI30 and the S&P 500. The exact horizon and cost treatment were not verified because of a paywall — [Springer, Electronic Markets 2025](https://link.springer.com/article/10.1007/s12525-025-00815-6) (abstract via search snippet)
- StockTwits sentiment predicts returns better than Reddit sentiment in a deep-learning setup (Nasekin and Chen 2020) — [Glasgow eprint](https://eprints.gla.ac.uk/214034/7/214034.pdf)
- Musk "One word: Doge" tweet: cumulative abnormal return about +8.17% within 5 minutes, peaking at +17.31% after 1 hour. Across 47 Musk crypto tweet events, effects were significant on average only for DOGE, not BTC — [Blockchain Research Lab (Ante)](https://www.blockchainresearchlab.org/2021/02/08/the-musk-effect-how-elon-musks-tweets-affect-the-cryptocurrency-market/); [Decrypt summary](https://decrypt.co/56598/study-shows-elon-musk-influences-bitcoin-and-dogecoin-prices)
- 2025 MDPI tweet study: negative tweets come with immediate volatility spikes, positive tweets have a delayed and lasting effect, and neutral tweets improve liquidity — [Data (MDPI) 2025](https://ideas.repec.org/a/gam/jdataj/v10y2025i4p50-d1626154.html)
- Dogecoin Granger test: one-way causality from Twitter sentiment to price at lags of 2 to 4 days, which is daily rather than intraday — [PMC10174546](https://www.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/pmc/articles/PMC10174546/)
- Kraaijeveld and De Smedt (2020, JIFMIM 65:101188) is widely cited on Twitter sentiment and crypto prices and on bots. A popular-press piece claims tweet volume led price more than sentiment did, and that bots posted mostly price info, ads and spam. I could not confirm that piece describes this paper — [citation in arXiv 2403.06036](https://arxiv.org/pdf/2403.06036); [Bitcoinist](https://bitcoinist.com/twitter-and-google-trends-interest-precedes-cryptocurrency-price-study-finds/amp/)
- Older algorithmic-trading evidence (Garcia and Schweitzer 2015): polarization of opinion plus exchange volume preceded BTC rises, and the strategy was profitable after costs. The data is pre-2015 and the market was much less efficient then — [Royal Society Open Science / PMC](https://www.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/pmc/articles/PMC4593685/)
- Student project combining Twitter, Google Trends and HF Coinbase data: BTC-USD Sharpe of 0.381 with 5 bps linear costs. Not peer reviewed — [Stanford MS&E 448 report](https://web.stanford.edu/class/msande448/2019/Final_reports/gr4.pdf)
- Weak-label fine-tuning of BERT improves the predictive value of text features for crypto returns (Ider and Lessmann). Horizon and out-of-sample numbers were not retrieved — [arXiv 2204.05781](https://arxiv.org/abs/2204.05781)
- A 2025 review describes the evidence as "mixed", with generalizability limited by noise in social media — [search summary; MDPI Mathematics 11/16/3441 related](https://www.mdpi.com/2227-7390/11/16/3441)

### Inferences
- At the 1h and shorter horizons relevant to us, published edge is either below fee size (StockTwits at 15 minutes) or depends on rare events from one account. Our round trip costs about 0.20 to 0.26% on USDT pairs, which likely exceeds any average tone-based edge on BTC, ETH or BNB.
- The most plausible use is a filter or risk gate, not an alpha source. Examples: avoid entries during sudden negative-tweet bursts, or treat abnormal mention volume as a volatility warning, mainly for PEPE and other memecoins where retail and influencer effects are largest. This duplicates what our RSS-headline GPT scoring and Fear and Greed index already cover.
- Reverse causality (price moves first, tweets follow) means a sentiment feature scored every 30 minutes will mostly echo the last 30 minutes of price.

### Gaps
- No 2024-2026 peer-reviewed study with minute-level out-of-sample returns net of costs for X sentiment was found.
- The full text of the Springer 2025 crowd-signals paper (horizon and costs) and the Kraaijeveld-De Smedt bot findings were not accessible.

## Q2. How can a bot get X/Twitter (and other social) crypto data cheaply in 2026?

### Takeaway
There is no free official X access. The X API is pay-per-use at $0.005 per post read, with about $20 of credit for new paying users. Scrapers that need no account (snscrape, Twint, Nitter) are dead. twscrape and Twikit work but need logged-in X accounts and break X's ToS. Third-party aggregators (LunarCrush, Santiment) lock social data behind paid plans. Within an hour the realistic free options are none for X itself, and possibly the CryptoPanic free API or Santiment's free key for limited social-volume data.

### Cited Findings
- Official X API pricing: post reads $0.005 per resource returned, owned reads $0.001, Counts:Recent $0.005 per request. The cap is 3M post reads per month, beyond that requires Enterprise. A $20 free credit incentive is mentioned for new paying users. No free tier is described — [docs.x.com pricing](https://docs.x.com/x-api/getting-started/pricing)
- X switched to pay-per-use on 2026-02-06. The free tier is effectively gone, and existing free users got a one-time $10 voucher (third-party report) — [roboin.io](https://roboin.io/article/en/2026/02/08/x-transitions-api-to-pay-per-use-model-ending-free-plan/); [Gigazine](https://gigazine.net/gsc_news/en/20260209-x-api-pay-per-use)
- Basic and Pro plans are closed to new sign-ups and remaining Basic users were migrated after 2026-06-01. Blogs disagree on the details — [postproxy.dev](https://www.postproxy.dev/blog/x-api-pricing-2026/); [wearefounders.uk](https://wearefounders.uk/post/the-x-api-price-hike-a-blow-to-indie-hackers)
- Scraper status (vendor blog, dated 2026): Nitter/XCancel offline, repo archived Aug 2026 after an X cease-and-desist (24 Aug 2026) citing the Texas Harmful Access by Computer Act and the Lanham Act. snscrape has not worked for X since mid-2023 (login wall). Twint archived March 2023. twscrape works (v0.20.1, 2026-08-25) with X accounts' auth_token/ct0 cookies. Twikit works intermittently with one account login — [xpoz.ai status guide](https://www.xpoz.ai/blog/guides/open-source-social-media-scrapers-what-still-works-2026/) (vendor with an interest; treat as indicative)
- twscrape's README notes that X's terms discourage multiple accounts. Account bans are a real risk — [xpoz.ai](https://www.xpoz.ai/blog/guides/open-source-social-media-scrapers-what-still-works-2026/); [twitterapi.io on account-pool cost](https://twitterapi.io/blog/twscrape)
- Reddit: unauthenticated `.json` endpoints have been blocked (HTTP 403) since late May 2026. The Data API is closing to new apps (register by 2026-09-30). Pushshift is moderators-only. Arctic Shift dumps still work but are monthly, so not real-time — [xpoz.ai](https://www.xpoz.ai/blog/guides/open-source-social-media-scrapers-what-still-works-2026/)
- LunarCrush API v4: the Hobby/free key covers market-data endpoints only, at 4 req/min and 100/day. Individual is 10/min and 2,000/day, Builder 100/min and 20,000/day, Scale 500/min and 100,000/day. Social, creator and AI endpoints are paid-only — [LunarCrush developer keys](https://lunarcrush.com/developers/keys). Pricing is Individual $90/mo, Builder $300, Scale $900 per the support page — [LunarCrush pricing FAQ](https://lunarcrush.com/support/how-much-does-lunarcrush-cost); a third-party site lists $30/mo (conflict, likely stale) — [toolmage](https://www.toolmage.com/en/tool/lunarcrush/). The free account is "extremely limited" — [LunarCrush support](https://lunarcrush.com/support/is-there-a-free-version-of-the-api)
- Santiment: has a free tier with limited history and calls (one third-party source says about 30 to 90 days). Paid plans start around $49/mo. Social-volume endpoints are callable with a free key — [n8n-nodes-santiment README](https://unpkg.com/@cryptodevops/n8n-nodes-santiment@0.3.9/README.md); [spark.money comparison](https://www.spark.money/tools/crypto-sentiment-tool-comparison) (both unofficial)
- CryptoPanic: plan tiers named developer, growth and enterprise. Filters include `bullish`, `bearish`, `hot`, `rising` and `important`. Search and panic_period are enterprise-only and page size is capped at 50 (from a third-party SDK). Official quotas could not be fetched (page is JS-rendered) — [cryptopanic-php SDK](https://packagist.org/packages/tigusigalpa/cryptopanic-php); [kukapay cryptopanic MCP server](https://cdn.jsdelivr.net/gh/kukapay/cryptopanic-mcp-server@main/README.md)

### Inferences
- Cost of the official X API for a minimal feed: 100 recent posts per coin for 7 coins every 30 minutes is 700 reads × $0.005 = $3.50 per pull, about $168 per day. That is far over budget. Even 20 posts per coin per hour is about $17 per day. The $20 credit would last only hours.
- twscrape needs a burner X account (account creation is something the user must do; the agent must not create accounts or enter passwords). It violates X's ToS, and the Nitter cease-and-desist shows X enforces against scrapers. Not recommended for a judged hackathon.
- The closest free signal is CryptoPanic, which is news plus community votes rather than tweets, and overlaps heavily with our existing RSS-headline scoring.
- Latency: scraped X data is near real-time when it works. Aggregator free tiers are delayed or coarse; exact delays were not found.

### Gaps
- Official CryptoPanic free-plan quotas and data delay for 2026 (pages are JS-rendered and could not be fetched).
- Santiment free-tier lag for social metrics; TheTie and Kaito (enterprise or contract only, no free tier found); CoinGecko and CoinMarketCap social fields were not checked. I believe CoinGecko removed community-data fields in 2024-25, but this is unverified.
- Telegram: no 2026 status found for Telethon-based collection.

## Q3. Which models classify crypto tweets well, and at what cost?

### Takeaway
CryptoBERT (ElKulako) is the standard free crypto-specific classifier: 3 classes, 128-token context, MIT license, CPU-runnable. FinTwitBERT and FinBERT are alternatives, but FinBERT was trained on formal financial news. Our existing GPT headline scorer is cheap enough to score about 1,000 short texts for under $0.02, so cost is not the bottleneck. Data access is.

### Cited Findings
- ElKulako/cryptobert: vinai/bertweet-base further trained on 3.2M crypto posts (StockTwits 1.875M, Telegram 664K, Twitter 496K, Reddit 172K). Fine-tuned on 2M balanced labeled StockTwits posts. Labels are Bearish, Neutral and Bullish. Max length 128 recommended, about 0.1B parameters, MIT license, about 389K downloads per month. The model card reports no accuracy or F1. Usage: `pipeline("text-classification", model="ElKulako/cryptobert")` — [HF model card](https://huggingface.co/ElKulako/cryptobert)
- FinTwitBERT (StephanAkkerman) builds on FinBERT and is trained on stock and crypto tweets; training code is at github.com/TimKoornstra/FinTwitBERT — [HF README](https://huggingface.co/StephanAkkerman/FinTwitBERT/raw/main/README.md)
- ProsusAI/finbert is used in tweet pipelines after stripping cashtags, URLs and mentions — [skillselion example](https://skillselion.com/skills/omer-metin/skills-for-antigravity/sentiment-analysis-trading)

### Inferences
- LLM cost from our own measured rate ($0.0005 per 30 headlines, about $0.000017 per item): scoring 1,000 tweets costs about $0.017. A $1 budget covers about 60K items. Batching 30 to 50 texts per call, asking for a JSON array of {coin, score -1..1}, and caching by text hash (spam and bot tweets are highly duplicated) is the cheap pattern. Dropping texts with more than 3 cashtags, URLs to airdrops, or duplicate text removes most bot spam before scoring.
- A CPU CryptoBERT inference at roughly 0.1B params handles hundreds of short texts per minute on a laptop (estimate, not benchmarked). This is a zero-cost option if we had a tweet stream.

### Gaps
- No head-to-head 2025-26 benchmark of CryptoBERT vs. GPT-class LLM scoring on crypto tweets with return-prediction outcomes was found.

If anything is added, a free CryptoPanic `filter=bullish/bearish` vote count per coin, or a "mention-spike" volatility gate for PEPE only, fed into the existing scorer, is the cheapest option.

## Q5 (priority add-on). Trump posts and Fed chair or FOMC events: reactions, free real-time feeds, repos, scoring and action rules, and the 2026-10-22/23 calendar

### Takeaway
Trump posts are the one social source with documented, large, fast crypto moves. Tariff or escalation posts cause sharp downside (Oct 10 2025: BTC down about 12 to 14%, with the cascade peaking about 6h after the post). Pro-crypto posts cause large alt spikes that fade within about 24h (Mar 2 2025: ADA +63%, XRP +32%, SOL +23% within about 2h, then down the next day). Fed and FOMC events mostly bring intraday volatility with frequent reversals, not a lasting direction. Trump posts are free and near real time via the trumpstruth.org RSS feed (verified working today). Fed posts are free via federalreserve.gov RSS (verified). There is no Fed chair speech or FOMC decision in our window. The next FOMC is Oct 27-28, after the challenge. A keyword prefilter plus GPT scoring as a risk gate (block entries or exit alts on tariff, war or hawkish posts) is buildable in about 1 to 2 hours for well under $0.05 of LLM spend.

### Inferences — cheap scoring and action design for our bot
- **Polling:** fetch `www.trumpstruth.org/feed` every 60 to 120s and dedupe by status id. Fetch `press_monetary.xml` and `speeches.xml` every 5 minutes. All free, no auth, under 1h to build by reusing the existing RSS-headline code.
- **Prefilter before the LLM:** score only posts matching `tariff|china|xi|trade deal|sanction|iran|hormuz|oil|war|strike|nuclear|fed|warsh|powell|rate|inflation|crypto|bitcoin|btc|ethereum|xrp|solana|bnb|pepe|reserve|stablecoin|gold`. At our measured ~$0.00002 per item, scoring every post costs under $0.01 a day.
- **Prompt (batch, JSON out):** `{"risk": -2..+2, "type": "tariff|geopolitical|fed|crypto_policy|other", "assets": [...], "novelty": 0-1, "confidence": 0-1}`. Tell the model that reposts or quotes of already-known policy (many posts are quotes such as "THE WALL STREET JOURNAL: ...") get novelty < 0.3.
- **Action rules (risk gate, not alpha):**
  - risk ≤ -1, type tariff or geopolitical, novelty ≥ 0.5: block new long entries for 2 to 4h and tighten stops on alts and PEPE. At risk = -2, exit alts to USDT. Rationale: on Oct 10 2025 the worst cascade came about 6h after the post, leaving time to exit at a 0.13% taker fee versus a 12 to 14% BTC drawdown.
  - crypto_policy, risk ≥ +1, naming a held or tradable coin: do not chase after the first 5 to 15 minutes, since the Mar 2025 spikes faded within 24h. At most a small entry with a time stop of 2h or less.
  - Fed: nothing scheduled in the window. If an unscheduled item appears on `press_monetary.xml`, block entries for 30 to 60 minutes because of reversal-prone volatility (K33).
- **Iran context:** today's Trump posts focus on Iran, oil and Hormuz. For a Wallex bot this matters mostly for USDT/IRT and local sentiment. On USDT pairs, only the global risk-off reading matters.
